# Cinnamon Story: The School Incident

**Format:** Single-player branching story for Cinnamon's `/story` command  
**Genre:** Comedy / Chaos / Mystery  
**Core mechanic:** Choices alter scenes and danger. Branches only merge at explicitly shared checkpoints.  
**Privacy:** Aryan is referred to only as Aryan; no surname.

## Story Rules

- Each player has an independent story state and can resume later.
- Danger begins at 0. Reaching 3 triggers the Bad Ending immediately.
- Route A adds 1 Danger. Route C adds 2 Danger. Routes B and D add none.
- Route-specific scenes must not be shown to players who did not take that route.
- All four opening routes converge at **Chapter 2 — The Investigation**.
- Later choices can change dialogue and ending availability.
- The story should not announce or reveal future route scenes before the player reaches them.

# Chapter 1 — The Wrong School

**Monday, 9:17 AM**

Cinnamon is bored. She has checked Discord 47 times, annoyed three members, and started an argument over whether tuna counts as breakfast.

Suddenly, an idea strikes.

**Cinnamon:** “WAIT. Aryan goes to school.”

**Member:** “And?”

**Cinnamon:** “WE’RE GOING TO VISIT HIM.”

She summons several server members and confidently marches toward a random school.

At the entrance:

**Guard:** “Students only. Why are you here?”

**Cinnamon:** “We're here to meet Aryan.”

**Guard:** “Which Aryan?”

**Cinnamon:** “THE Aryan.”

**Guard:** “I don't know any Aryan.”

## Opening Choice

### A — Principal's Son
Cinnamon claims Aryan is the principal's son.  
**Danger: +1**  
Go to **Route A — The Principal's Son**.

### B — Call Aryan
Cinnamon asks the guard to call the school office and check for Aryan.  
**Danger: +0**  
Go to **Route B — Call Aryan**.

### C — Sneak Inside
Cinnamon attempts to enter without permission.  
**Danger: +2**  
Go to **Route C — Sneak Inside**.

### D — Cat Inspector
Cinnamon claims she's inspecting the school's cat population.  
**Danger: +0**  
Go to **Route D — Cat Inspector**.

# Route A — The Principal's Son

## A1 — The Office

The guard calls the principal. Five minutes later, Cinnamon is sitting in his office, with her members lined up behind her.

**Principal:** “I don't have a son named Aryan.”

**Cinnamon:** “That's exactly what someone hiding their son would say.”

**Principal:** “WHAT?”

**Cinnamon:** “Nothing.”

The principal calls a teacher to escort everyone out. The teacher briefly steps away to answer another call.

Cinnamon looks at the door.

**Cinnamon:** “Members.”

**Member:** “No.”

**Cinnamon:** “I haven't said anything.”

**Member:** “Your face has.”

## A2 — Escape

Cinnamon gets up. A member accidentally knocks over a stack of papers.

The teacher returns.

**Teacher:** “HEY!”

Cinnamon runs into the corridor.

**Member:** “WHY ARE WE RUNNING?!”

**Cinnamon:** “BECAUSE WE'RE FREE!”

They reach the courtyard and stop.

**Member:** “We escaped the principal's office.”

**Cinnamon:** “I was never detained.”

**Member:** “YOU WERE LITERALLY IN HIS OFFICE.”

**Route A ends here.** Proceed to **Chapter 2 — The Investigation**.

# Route B — Call Aryan

## B1 — The Receptionist

The guard calls the school office.

**Receptionist:** “There is no student named Aryan here.”

**Cinnamon:** “Check again.”

**Receptionist:** “We did.”

**Cinnamon:** “Check emotionally.”

The receptionist hangs up.

**Member:** “Maybe Aryan doesn't study here?”

**Cinnamon:** “Impossible.”

**Member:** “You literally picked a random school.”

**Cinnamon:** “I PICKED WITH CONFIDENCE.”

Cinnamon decides to investigate the student directory.

**Route B ends here.** Proceed to **Chapter 2 — The Investigation**.

# Route C — Sneak Inside

## C1 — Operation Aryan

Cinnamon looks at her members.

**Cinnamon:** “Operation Aryan.”

**Member:** “This is a terrible idea.”

**Cinnamon:** “Noted. Proceed.”

They attempt to enter without permission. A teacher notices almost immediately.

**Teacher:** “STOP!”

Everyone freezes.

**Cinnamon:** “We are students.”

**Teacher:** “WHICH CLASS?”

**Cinnamon:** “Cat class.”

The teacher escorts them to the principal's office.

## C2 — The Consequences

**Principal:** “First you claim to be visiting my son. Then you attempt to enter without permission.”

**Cinnamon:** “When you say it like that, it sounds bad.”

**Principal:** “BECAUSE IT IS BAD.”

Cinnamon's members sit silently. One whispers:

**Member:** “We should've selected B.”

Cinnamon hears.

**Cinnamon:** “I WILL REMEMBER THAT.”

The principal eventually orders everyone to leave.

Outside:

**Cinnamon:** “Okay. New plan.”

**Member:** “Please let it be a good one.”

**Cinnamon:** “We find Aryan.”

**Route C ends here.** Proceed to **Chapter 2 — The Investigation**.

# Route D — Cat Inspector

## D1 — Professional Inspection

**Guard:** “Students only.”

**Cinnamon:** “I'm here to inspect the school's cat population.”

**Guard:** “We don't have cats.”

**Cinnamon:** “That's precisely why the inspection is necessary.”

A teacher overhears.

**Teacher:** “Animal inspection?”

**Cinnamon:** “Professional.”

The teacher escorts Cinnamon toward the courtyard.

## D2 — The Cats

They find three cats sleeping near a garden. Cinnamon opens a notebook.

**Cinnamon:** “Cat one: sleeping.”

**Member:** “Cat two: also sleeping.”

**Cinnamon:** “Cat three: suspicious.”

The third cat opens one eye. Cinnamon immediately steps backward.

**Cinnamon:** “THIS INDIVIDUAL IS DANGEROUS.”

The members take imaginary inspection notes. Eventually, Cinnamon remembers why she came.

**Cinnamon:** “WAIT. ARYAN.”

**Route D ends here.** Proceed to **Chapter 2 — The Investigation**.

# Chapter 2 — The Investigation

**Shared checkpoint: all four routes converge here.**

Cinnamon and her members eventually arrive at the student directory.

**Member:** “Okay. Let's actually check whether Aryan studies here.”

Cinnamon searches:

`ARYAN`

*Searching...*

**Directory:** 0 results.

**Cinnamon:** “Search again.”

`ARYAN`

**Directory:** 0 results.

**Cinnamon:** “Search emotionally.”

**Member:** “THAT IS NOT HOW DATABASES WORK.”

Cinnamon searches the entire directory.

No Aryan. Not in any class.

**Member:** “Cinnamon.”

**Cinnamon:** “What?”

**Member:** “I think you brought us to the wrong school.”

**Cinnamon:** “Impossible.”

She finally calls Aryan.

---

# Chapter 3 — The Revelation

**Aryan:** “Hello?”

**Cinnamon:** “WHERE ARE YOU?”

**Aryan:** “Home?”

Silence.

**Cinnamon:** “WHAT?!”

**Aryan:** “Why?”

**Cinnamon:** “WE'RE AT YOUR SCHOOL.”

**Aryan:** “Which school?”

Cinnamon slowly looks at the building, the student directory, and her members.

**Cinnamon:** “...”

**Aryan:** “Cinnamon?”

**Cinnamon:** “YOU DON'T EVEN GO HERE?!”

**Aryan:** “I NEVER SAID I DID.”

The members burst into laughter.

**Member:** “WE CAME TO THE WRONG SCHOOL.”

**Cinnamon:** “I WILL REMEMBER THIS.”

**Aryan:** “Remember what? You came to the wrong school.”

# Chapter 4 — The Return

Cinnamon must decide how to handle the humiliation.

## Final Choice

### A — Blame Aryan
**Cinnamon:** “This is entirely your fault.”

**Aryan:** “HOW?”

**Cinnamon:** “You failed to attend the school I imagined for you.”

### B — Planned Reconnaissance
**Cinnamon:** “This was a reconnaissance mission.”

**Member:** “For what?”

**Cinnamon:** “Future tuna operations.”

### C — Apologize
Cinnamon returns to the principal to apologize.

**Cinnamon:** “I apologize.”

**Principal:** “For what?”

**Cinnamon:** “Everything.”

**Principal:** “Good.”

### D — Immediate Departure
Cinnamon marches out.

**Cinnamon:** “Nobody speaks of this again.”

**Member:** “I'm posting it in general.”

**Cinnamon:** “NO.”

# Endings

## Good Ending — The Legendary Cat
Cinnamon admits the mistake. Everyone leaves peacefully. Aryan laughs at the entire server.

## Normal Ending — Administrative Disaster
The principal remembers Cinnamon forever. Aryan refuses to answer her calls for ten minutes.

## Bad Ending — The Principal's Nemesis
If Danger reaches 3, the story ends immediately.

**Principal:** “YOU ARE BANNED FROM THIS SCHOOL.”

**Cinnamon:** “I WAS NEVER ENROLLED.”

**GAME OVER**

## Secret Ending — The Cat Distribution System
Available if Cinnamon discovers the courtyard cats and makes the required good choices.

She adopts a mysterious school cat as the server mascot.

Aryan gets replaced as the server's favorite.

**Aryan:** “I'M ACTUALLY LEAVING.”

## Implementation Notes

- Store route selection, Danger, and ending flags per player.
- A player who selected A or C sees only that route's office/escape/consequence scenes.
- B and D must not show the office escape scene.
- Route A's escape is a branch-only scene; Route C's office visit is a separate consequence scene.
- Merge all routes at Chapter 2, not before.
- Danger 3 triggers the Bad Ending immediately.
- Keep the secret ending gated behind route/choice flags.
- Support save/resume for each player.
